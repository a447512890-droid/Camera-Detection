Destination Risk Check

Destination

Vancouver

Low Risk · Level 1

Security Level

Overall Risk: Low (Level 1‑General Caution)

Generally safe for ordinary travellers with a low level of violent crime, though conditions vary significantly across different areas.

High‑risk areas: Parts of Downtown Eastside and Gastown. Street loitering, aggressive panhandling and minor public‑order issues may occur here.

Common property‑related risks: Vehicle theft, pickpocketing and shoplifting are the most frequent incidents. Risks are concentrated in popular tourist sites, commercial districts and parking lots. Blessing scams targeting Chinese nationals have increased notably in Vancouver in recent years.

Natural‑Hazard Alerts

Natural‑Hazard Level: Moderate Risk (Level 2‑Stay Alert)

The area lies within an earthquake‑prone zone with potential risks of damaging earthquakes. Summer wildfire smoke from external regions may cause poor air quality.

Occasional extreme heat and coastal flooding triggered by heavy rainfall can also occur.

These hazards have low probability under normal conditions, yet may significantly disrupt travel once activated. Please follow local weather and hazard alerts and take basic precautions.

Scam & Violent‑Incident Alerts

Violent incidents are relatively rare in Vancouver. Major risks mainly involve property‑targeted scams and street thefts.

Common scam: Blessing scams targeting Chinese nationals have increased notably in recent years. Scammers approach victims with claims of spiritual blessing or removing misfortune. They trick victims into handing over cash and jewellery for fake rituals and steal valuables through sleight‑of‑hand. Such incidents frequently occur in Chinatown, Gastown and other tourist‑heavy areas. Walk away immediately if approached. Never give your valuables to strangers.

Frequent property crimes: Pickpocketing, vehicle theft and shoplifting are common, especially at tourist attractions, commercial districts and parking lots. Lock your vehicle securely and do not leave valuables inside.

Aggressive panhandlers may be encountered on streets. Avoid unnecessary conversations to prevent disputes.

Local Customs & Cultural Taboos

Vancouver is a multicultural immigrant city that values personal privacy, equality and inclusiveness. Please remain courteous and avoid loud talking in public areas.

Always ask for permission before photographing strangers, Indigenous ceremonies, totem poles and cultural exhibits. Photography or video recording may be forbidden at certain Indigenous sacred sites and ritual events; please follow on‑site signs. Do not touch Indigenous cultural artifacts without permission.

Tipping is customary locally. A 15‑20% tip is expected for restaurant and hotel services. No tip is required for convenience‑store or fast‑food purchases.

Smoking is banned in all indoor public spaces. Cannabis consumption is also prohibited in parks, streets and other public areas, and violations may result in fines. On escalators, stand on the right to allow others to pass on the left.

Respect diverse ethnic groups, religions and lifestyles. Avoid asking personal questions about income or marital status. Comply with dress codes and rules when visiting religious venues.
